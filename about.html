About Me

I am a Software Engineering and Cybersecurity student at École Supérieure de Technologie de Nador (ESTN), Morocco.

My interests lie in offensive security, particularly web application security and penetration testing. I enjoy understanding how applications and systems work, exploring vulnerabilities, and improving my skills through CTF competitions and hands-on practice.

Technical Interests

  • Penetration Testing: Learning enumeration, exploitation, and privilege escalation techniques.
  • CTF Competitions: Solving challenges in Web, Pwn, Reverse Engineering, Cryptography, and OSINT.
